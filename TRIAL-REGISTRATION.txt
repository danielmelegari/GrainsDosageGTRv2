GRAINSDOSAGE - REGISTERED WINDOWS TRIAL

This trial works only with a signed license for your computer.

1. Open "GrainsDosage Trial Registration" from the Start menu.
2. Choose Yes to save your machine request on the Desktop.
3. Send GrainsDosage-machine-request.json to the publisher.
4. After receiving trial.license, open the registration tool again.
5. Choose No, select your license, and reload the plugin in your DAW.

The license has a fixed start date and expires exactly 14 days later.
The registration tool shows both dates. Before activation or after expiry,
the trial plugin produces no audio. It does not close or modify your DAW.
A license issued for another computer will not work here.

No internet connection is required. Reinstalling does not extend the trial.
